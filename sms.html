SMS Terms

R2 Fabrication, LLC order and delivery text notifications

Program description

R2 Fabrication, LLC sends order-specific text notifications, such as order status and delivery updates, to customers who opt in.

Message frequency

About 3–5 messages per order or delivery. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Text STOP at any time to cancel. You will receive one confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent. Text HELP for help, or email info@TeamR2.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We will not share your information with third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text messaging services. See our Privacy Policy.
